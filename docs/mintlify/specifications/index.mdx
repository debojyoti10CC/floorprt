The original `docs/*.md` and decision log are preserved and mirrored here by `scripts/docs-generate.mjs`. Their content is not rewritten to pretend proposals were executed. MDX-sensitive characters are escaped for rendering; original Markdown remains available through each page's source link.

## How to read them

1. [Mechanism and proofs](/specifications/MECHANISM_AND_PROOFS) defines the non-negotiable payout invariant.
2. [Implementation decisions](/specifications/IMPLEMENTATION_DECISIONS) resolves known blueprint ambiguities and scope extensions.
3. Current guides and compiler-derived references describe actual interfaces.
4. [Release evidence](/specifications/RELEASE_EVIDENCE) and [features/limits](/specifications/FEATURES_AND_LIMITATIONS) separate demonstrated execution from future plans.
5. PRD, architecture, contract/API drafts and build plans retain proposed signatures, technologies and milestones. Do not treat them as production integration references.

Research/source audits document architectural inspiration, provenance and their inspection limits. Sponsor planning is historical eligibility research, not a prize qualification or legal assurance. Recheck official rules independently.

See [source reconciliation](/maintenance/source-audit) for differences found while documenting the implementation.
