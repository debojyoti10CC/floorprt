**Research date: 6 November 2026**

AQRA's active backtest is Polymarket-only. It currently covers 3,906 wallet histories, 3,647,433 fill rows, 1,025,654 daily P&L observations, 15/15 scheduled month-boundary block checks, 6,155 candidate wallet-month rows and 11 completed forward rounds. No other venue rows have been added to those counts.

Other venues can add breadth, but raw trader rows must not be pooled until their currency, account identity, cash flows, settlement history, fees, timestamps and data-use rights are comparable. A larger but mismatched sample makes the credit claim weaker.

## Venue access and role

| Venue | Trader history | Access requirements | AQRA treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Polymarket | Public wallet P&L, positions, activity and trades | Address may be a proxy or deposit wallet | Current benchmark and borrower evidence source |
| OPINION | Filled wallet trades include fee, quote token and USD value; positions expose open holdings | API key; wallet lookup; paginated history | Priority cash-market adapter; validate fees, settlement and cutoff coverage |
| Predict.fun | Address-level fill matches, positions, account activity; BNB trade, resolution and settlement events | API key; smart-account mapping; third-party indexed archive starts 22 Oct 2026 | Priority short-window cash cohort; do not claim a 12-month history |
| Limitless | Account positions, trades and portfolio history | Owner-authenticated HMAC API | Opt-in account connection, not a public trader census |
| Kalshi | Public markets; private member portfolio and trades | Signed member key; account-specific data and use restrictions | Member-consented data only; no anonymous population query |
| Gemini Predictions | Public events and aggregate volume; account positions and history are private | Authenticated API/OAuth | Opt-in account adapter after history and settlement completeness review |
| Manifold | Public bets, accounts and portfolio history | M$ virtual currency; free bulk data is non-commercial and updated only through July 2024 | Separate behavioral cohort; never cash credit evidence |
| Prediction.com | Matched events, market prices and price history across venues | API key; market-data API, not account trade histories | Market discovery/matching only; not a new trader cohort |
| Bitquery | Decoded on-chain fills, resolutions and settlements for supported chains | Provider API; Predict.fun BNB data has no archive before 22 Oct 2026 | Independent reconciliation; deduplicate against venue APIs |

## Why the data stay separated

The Polymarket score uses completed monthly activity, realized market P&L, turnover, trade count and drawdown. Other venues may expose live marks, virtual currency, incomplete deposits, incentive income, distinct quote tokens, or only the requesting account's history. Those are not equivalent observations.

The collection contract for each adapter requires a stable consented account identifier, trade/fill timestamps, outcome and side, cash-denominated costs and fees, settlement state, current open-position marks, deposits/withdrawals, raw source references and a reproducible as-of cutoff. The analysis must preserve raw source units and evaluate each venue separately using temporal splits and trader-clustered uncertainty.

## Collection order

1. Preserve the existing Polymarket manifest and benchmark.
2. Capture a short-window Predict.fun cohort and an OPINION cohort as separate datasets. Obtain each API key, paginate fully, retain raw responses, and reconcile a sample of fills, fees and outcomes to the chain.
3. Add borrower-initiated Limitless, Kalshi and Gemini account history only with scoped authorization; never request a password or expose a private API key in browser code.
4. Use Manifold for a separately labeled virtual-currency behavioral check. Obtain a commercial license before product use.
5. Use Prediction.com for event matching and market histories and Bitquery for chain-level reconciliation, not as additional independent trader populations.
