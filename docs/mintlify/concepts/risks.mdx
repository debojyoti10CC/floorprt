<Warning>
AQRA is a research prototype. Do not use it to make real lending decisions or deposit real-value assets. The trader score is not a probability of default, and the contracts have not been audited.
</Warning>

## Credit and loss

The benchmark measures historical trader activity and next-month realized P&L. It contains no borrower repayment or default labels. A wallet passing the research screen can still fail to repay, and the score does not establish income, assets, identity, or ability to pay.

Loans are direct and bond-backed. At the demo terms of $100 principal, $102 due and a $25 bond, a lender can lose up to $75 of principal even if the full bond is recovered. Bond transfer failure, token loss, contract defects or other execution problems can make recovery lower. No shared pool or funded loss reserve exists.

## Data and identity

Public market records can be incomplete, delayed, revised or incorrectly attributed. A linked ENS name is a discovery hint, not proof that one person controls every address. Wallet consent must be current and scoped to the request. Public wallet histories may reveal sensitive behavior even when addresses are pseudonymous.

## Execution and infrastructure

The local walkthrough uses Anvil impersonation and test USDC. It demonstrates contract state transitions, not borrower authorization or public-chain settlement. On a configured network, verify chain ID, token, contract addresses, transaction status and event data before relying on a result. RPC or API outages can interrupt evidence retrieval and transaction submission.

The Chainlink CRE workflow and Arkiv data path are integration code, not evidence of a successful live workflow or public write. Sponsor eligibility and product readiness must be judged from verifiable execution artifacts.

## Product boundary

AQRA does not custody prediction-market positions, enforce future trading profits, guarantee repayment, insure lenders, or provide a calibrated credit rating. The benchmark supports research into candidate-selection policies only.
