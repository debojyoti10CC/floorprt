## Mint, merge, redeem

`OutcomeMarketFactory` creates one approved `OutcomeTokens` issuer in its constructor. The issuer binds one registry and six-decimal collateral contract. No privileged free-claim mint exists.

```text
mintCompleteSet(market, q): take q × 1000000 collateral; mint q YES + q NO
mergeCompleteSet(market, q): burn q YES + q NO; return q × 1000000
redeem(market, side, q): burn caller's claims; pay fixed terminal payout
```

Mint and merge require unresolved state **strictly before observation time**. Mint also checks new-collateral eligibility. Merge remains possible for a disabled market if its observation is still open. Redemption requires FINALIZED or CANCELLED state, burns even losing claims and reduces per-market escrow by actual payout.

The issuer verifies collateral received by balance delta on mint. Fee-on-transfer underfunding reverts atomically. Six decimals alone do not make an arbitrary token acceptable; deployment must select approved transferable collateral.

## Token IDs

For market ID m:

```text
YES = 2 × m + 1
NO  = 2 × m + 2
```

For markets 2 and 3, the canonical pledged claims are YES token 5 and NO token 8. The counterpart tokens 6 and 7 remain in the wallet.

## Acquisition cost is separate

Minting one complete set in **each** of two markets costs 2 USDC and produces four claims. Pledging the selected pair certifies its one-USDC floor; the two counterpart claims remain unpledged. The certified floor is not proof of profit on the full acquisition strategy.

## Vault safety boundary

`lockPair` recomputes the proof, records immutable owner/beneficiary/quantities, and pulls an exact ERC-1155 batch. The receiver accepts only the known issuer, vault operator and expected sender/IDs/amounts/data context. Single or unsolicited batch deposits revert.

Only the bound desk can encumber, redeem or release repaid collateral. The desk is bound once and must point back to the same vault. There is no admin collateral-rescue or upgrade route.

- OPEN: owner may release the original inventory.
- ENCUMBERED: owner cannot withdraw; one nonzero loan lien applies.
- RELEASED: original claims returned to owner after unfunded release or full repayment.
- REDEEMED: pledged claims burned and cash sent to the desk for settlement.

`redeemFor` always burns **the caller's** claims, even with an ERC-1155 operator approval; it cannot burn another holder's inventory and redirect proceeds.
