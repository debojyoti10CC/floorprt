---
title: 'Fixed debt and the settlement waterfall'
description: 'Borrower consent, conservative debt limits and lender-first distributions.'
---

A borrower publishes the **exact principal**, maximum total due and request expiry for an OPEN position. A distinct lender funds all of that principal or nothing; partial funding is not supported. Lender identity must differ from owner, beneficiary and vault.

## Debt cap

```text
D = principal + fixed lender fee
reserve = ceil(F × bufferBps / 10000)
maximumDue = F − reserve
D ≤ borrowerMaximumDue
D ≤ maximumDue
```

The deployed script selects `bufferBps = 2000` (20%). The constructor makes this immutable and permits values from 0 through 10,000; integrations must read the deployment's actual value rather than assuming a UI label.

For `F = 1000000`, reserve is `200000`, maximum due `800000`, sample principal `750000` and sample due `780000`. The fee is `30000`. The reserve is **headroom in the payout**, not escrowed insurance or a protocol-owned reserve pool.

Request expiry must be in the future and no later than observation time. At expiry itself funding is rejected. New requests/funding also require unresolved observation strictly before its timestamp.

## Funding

In one transaction, `CreditDesk` checks consent, state, deadline, exact principal and due; deactivates the request; records the loan; encumbers the position; and transfers principal directly from lender to owner. A failed transfer rolls all changes back. Due does not accrue with time.

## Settlement

After common finalization or cancellation, anyone may call `settle(loanId)`:

1. Mark the funded loan settled.
2. Redeem the vault's exact lower YES and upper NO claims.
3. Require exact cash delta, redeemed amount at least certified floor, and redeemed amount at least due.
4. Transfer fixed due to lender.
5. Transfer residual to the immutable position beneficiary.

```text
lenderPaid = D
beneficiaryPaid = redeemed − D
```

A shortfall **reverts**, rather than reporting a successful partial repayment. State updates and transfers are atomic.

| Scenario | Redeemed | Lender | Beneficiary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Inside, including lower equality | 2.00 | 0.78 | 1.22 |
| Outside, including upper equality | 1.00 | 0.78 | 0.22 |
| Common cancellation | 1.00 | 0.78 | 0.22 |

## Repayment alternative

The borrower may pay **all fixed due**, including the full fee, and receive the exact pledged claims back. No partial repayment, time discount or fee prorating exists. Repayment remains available after maturity while funded. Whichever of settlement or repayment succeeds first prevents the other.

No mark-to-market margin calls or liquidation engine exists in P0. Oracle delays still create liquidity/time risk.
