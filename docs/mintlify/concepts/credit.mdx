AQRA's active product is a direct lender and borrower flow. Public settled prediction-market history is an experimental screen. A borrower posts the escrowed bond; a distinct lender decides whether to advance the principal and accepts the uncovered shortfall.

## What is enforceable

- The request binds the borrower, evidence digest, fixed terms and linked-wallet consent.
- The bond is held in the repayment token until repayment, cancellation or matured default.
- One distinct lender funds the fixed principal. Funding is not pooled or automatic.
- Full repayment before the deadline returns the bond and pays the fixed due.
- A matured unpaid loan can transfer the bond to the lender and record the principal shortfall.

## Current demonstration terms

| Principal | Total due | Bond | Shortfall after full bond recovery |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| $100 | $102 | $25 | $75 |

If the bond token loses value or a token/contract/operational failure prevents recovery, the shortfall can be greater. Trading P&L is not pledged, seized or promised to the lender.

## Separate from credit proof

The performance score is not a probability of default, repayment forecast, loan approval or collateral. PD and default-dependence inputs on the Risk engine page are assumptions. AQRA's pooled-credit gate stays rejected until matured loan outcomes, validated PD and committed capital exist.
