---
title: 'Performance-metrics-based lending'
description: 'How trader performance gates pilot eligibility and lenders review bonded loan requests.'
---

AQRA's pilot uses dated prediction-market metrics—activity, repeat profitability, realized P&L efficiency and drawdown—to determine whether a borrower meets the request policy. A qualifying borrower escrows a bond and receives an attested report. The lender reloads the metric inputs, checks the evidence digest, and decides whether to fund.

## What is enforceable

- The request binds the borrower, evidence digest, fixed terms and linked-wallet consent.
- The policy score gates a request; it does not set the rate or prove repayment ability.
- The lender app enables funding only after the loaded metric packet matches the attested digest.
- The bond is held in the repayment token until repayment, cancellation or matured default.
- One distinct lender funds the fixed principal. Funding is not pooled or automatic.
- Full repayment before the deadline returns the bond and pays the fixed due.
- A matured unpaid loan can transfer the bond to the lender and record the principal shortfall.

If the bond token loses value or a token/contract/operational failure prevents recovery, the shortfall can be greater. Trading P&L is not pledged, seized or promised to the lender.

## Separate from credit proof

The performance score is the pilot's eligibility input, not a probability of default, repayment forecast or collateral. PD and default-dependence inputs on the Risk engine page are assumptions. The benchmark measures next-month trader P&L, and the simple monthly-P&L ranking (73.6%) exceeds the consistency policy (70.0%). AQRA's pooled-credit gate stays rejected until matured loan outcomes, validated PD and committed capital exist.
