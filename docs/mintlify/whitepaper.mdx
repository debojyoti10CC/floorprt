# AQRA — Performance-Metrics-Based Lending Protocol

**Version 0.1 · 6 November 2026**

## Abstract

AQRA is a prediction-market performance-metrics-based lending protocol pilot. It uses dated activity, repeat profitability, realized P&L efficiency and drawdown to gate a bonded loan request. A lender can reload those inputs, verify the evidence digest matches the attested report, and choose whether to fund.

The benchmark evaluates future trader P&L, not loan repayment. The metric policy does not set pricing, prove ability to repay or protect principal. The lender can lose beyond the posted bond.

## Protocol

1. **Build evidence.** AQRA reads settled Polymarket wallet history through a dated cutoff and records its evidence digest.
2. **Prove account control.** A borrower may group up to four wallets. ENS helps discover addresses; each linked controller must consent to the request.
3. **Request credit.** The borrower selects principal, fixed repayment and term, then escrows a first-loss bond.
4. **Attest and fund.** A pinned report sender attests to the policy result. A separate lender reviews the terms and funds the exact request.
5. **Repay or record default.** Repayment returns the bond. At maturity, a default can transfer the bond to the lender and records any remaining shortfall.

The trader-credit desk does not custody or liquidate native Polymarket positions. Wallet history is evidence for screening, not collateral. The separate outcome-token protocol is a local test demonstration and does not establish custody of external prediction-market claims.

## Underwriting policy

Policy `aqra-trader-credit-v1` evaluates the three completed UTC months before the cutoff. A month is active at $500 or more of turnover and at least 20 trades. Eligibility requires two active months, two positive active months, positive combined realized P&L, activity in the latest month and a score of at least 60 out of 100.

The score allocates up to 40 points to consistency, 35 to realized efficiency and 25 to observed drawdown. Missing or unaudited history is held from funding. A score is the pilot's deterministic eligibility input, tied to the policy version and evidence digest. It does not set principal or fee terms and is not a calibrated credit rating.

## Benchmark design

The Data page uses monthly walk-forward rounds. At each decision cutoff, candidate selection uses only earlier observations. Outcomes are evaluated in the following month and show realized trader P&L and activity. They are not loan returns.

The expanded sample schedules 768 BTC/ETH 15-minute market sessions across November 2025–October 2026; 764 resolved markets were captured. It includes 3,906 wallet histories, 3,647,433 fill rows, 1,025,654 daily P&L observations, 15/15 audited month-boundary blocks, 6,155 candidate wallet-month rows and 11 completed forward rounds.

| Selection policy | Positive next-month P&L | Evaluated wallet-months |
|---|---:|---:|
| Consistency screen | 154/220 · 70.0% | 220 |
| Largest monthly P&L | 162/220 · 73.6% | 220 |
| 10× to below 100× settled buy | 55/160 · 34.4% | 160 |
| At least 100× settled buy | 2/4 · 50.0% | 4 |
| Seeded sample baseline | 61/220 · 27.7% | 220 |

The consistency screen is 42.3 percentage points above the seeded baseline and 3.6 points below the largest-monthly-P&L comparator. Wallet-month events may repeat addresses and are not independent borrower observations. No statistical significance or causal effect is claimed.

The contract demo advances $100 with $102 due and a $25 bond. This is a test term, not a performance result. The contract supports voluntary repayment and bond-only recovery at default; it cannot collect future trading P&L. Mature repayment/default labels are unavailable.

Polymarket daily P&L is realized market P&L, not account ROI; starting bankroll and full account equity are unavailable. High-multiple settled buys are exploratory cohorts and do not prove repeatable performance.

## Credit stress

The separate risk engine reports principal loss after the bond, expected loss, a correlated loss distribution, VaR, Expected Shortfall, reserve gap and scenario margin. PD, correlation, bond recovery and cost inputs are assumptions. The app keeps pooled approval rejected until repayment outcomes, out-of-time validation and committed capital exist. See [Risk engine specification](/specifications/risk-engine) for formulas and calibration requirements.

Coverage, policy comparisons, wallet-level evidence and the current dataset commitment are shown in the app’s Data tab. The sample is not a venue-wide census; missing account equity, bankroll and open-position history prevent ROI claims.

## Data and integrations

- **Polymarket** public P&L and trade sources provide wallet-level observations. Boundary blocks are recorded so a decision has a verifiable as-of point.
- **ENS** names and text records help discover wallet groups. A name alone does not prove control; linked wallets sign scoped consent.
- **Chainlink CRE** source can recompute evidence for an authenticated report. A successful official CLI simulation or live workflow deployment is not yet recorded. Local demo reports are explicitly labeled local; neither is a public DON execution claim.
- **Arkiv** can hold an expiring packet so lenders can discover a request and verify its claims against the loan record. Publishing requires the borrower’s wallet and network fees.

## Risk and limits

- The lender can lose principal beyond the bond. A high score does not guarantee repayment.
- Prediction-market history may be incomplete, account-specific or affected by selection and survivorship bias.
- The application is a local or testnet prototype using test funds. It has no independent security audit or legal clearance.
- There is no production liquidity pool, automatic loan auction, real-market position custody or guaranteed liquidation.
