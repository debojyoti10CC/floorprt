## Abstract

AQRA uses a trader's dated prediction-market performance as an eligibility signal for a small, bonded loan request. The borrower supplies wallet history, reviews a cutoff and score, and signs a fixed-term request with a USDC bond. A separate lender verifies the report and terms before funding. The benchmark measures future trader P&L, not willingness or ability to repay. The score is an auditable screening signal, not a validated credit score.

The current contract demo advances $100 with $102 due and a $25 bond. At full bond recovery, up to $75 of principal remains exposed. AQRA has no mature loan repayment labels or production lending pool.

## Protocol workflow

1. **Build dated evidence.** Read public Polymarket account history through the last completed month and record an evidence digest.
2. **Establish wallet control.** ENS helps discover linked wallets; every linked controller must provide scoped consent.
3. **Request credit.** The borrower chooses principal, fixed repayment and term, then escrows the bond.
4. **Publish a lender-verifiable report.** After explicit consent, upload the report bytes to Swarm and commit their hash/reference to the lending contract. Publish an expiring discovery pointer on Arkiv Rabdi.
5. **Review and fund.** A lender retrieves the exact report, checks its hash and recomputed evidence against the lending contract, acknowledges the shortfall and funds the request.
6. **Repay or settle default.** Repayment returns the bond. A matured default transfers only the contract-held bond.

Polymarket history is read-only evidence. AQRA does not sign in to Polymarket, trade for the borrower, custody its account or pledge native Polymarket positions. A Privy wallet connection is not Polymarket OAuth and cannot by itself prove that a connected signer owns a different proxy/deposit account.

## Underwriting policy

Policy `aqra-trader-credit-v1` evaluates the three completed UTC months before the cutoff. A month is active at turnover of at least $500 and at least 20 trades. A request requires two active months, two positive active months, positive combined realized P&L, activity in the most recent month and a score of at least 60/100.

The deterministic score assigns up to 40 points to profitable-month consistency, 35 to realized P&L efficiency and 25 to observed drawdown. It gates eligibility only; it does not set the fee or principal, establish recovery or estimate probability of default.

## Polymarket walk-forward benchmark

The expanded capture covers November 2025–October 2026. It scheduled 768 BTC/ETH 15-minute market sessions and retained 764 resolved captures. The dataset contains 3,906 wallet histories, 3,647,433 fill rows, 1,025,654 daily P&L observations, 15/15 scheduled month-boundary block checks, 6,155 candidate wallet-month rows and 11 completed forward rounds.

Each monthly decision uses only data before that cutoff. The next-month outcome is positive realized trader P&L, not loan repayment.

| Selection rule | Positive next-month P&L | Rows | 95% Wilson interval* |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Consistency screen | 70.0% | 154/220 | 63.6–75.7% |
| Largest monthly P&L | 73.6% | 162/220 | 67.4–79.0% |
| 10× to below 100× settled buy | 34.4% | 55/160 | 27.5–42.0% |
| 100× or higher settled buy | 50.0% | 2/4 | 15.0–85.0% |
| Seeded sample baseline | 27.7% | 61/220 | 22.2–34.0% |

The consistency screen exceeds the seeded baseline by 42.3 percentage points, but trails the largest-monthly-P&L rank by 3.6 points. Their row-level intervals overlap. Wallet-months can repeat a trader and are dependent; the intervals are descriptive and ignore clustering. No significance or causal effect is claimed. The 100× cohort has only four observations.

Daily realized P&L is not account ROI because opening equity, bankroll and complete cash flows are unavailable. None of these statistics is a loan-repayment result. High-multiple settled buys do not prove repeatable skill.

## Cross-market data study

Only Polymarket is currently collected by AQRA's benchmark engine. Additional sources can increase observations, but do not belong in one credit cohort until cash semantics, timestamped completeness, fees, identity linkage, and data-use rights are verified.

| Venue | Trader-level access | Current AQRA treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Polymarket | Public wallet P&L, positions, activity and trades | Current benchmark and borrower evidence source |
| OPINION | Filled wallet trades include fees, quote-token and USD amount; positions expose holdings | Priority cash adapter; validate pagination, settlement and cutoff |
| Predict.fun | Wallet-address fills, positions, activity and BNB settlement events; API key required | Priority short-window cohort; third-party indexed archive starts 22 Oct 2026, so not a year-long replication |
| Limitless | Owner-authenticated positions, trades and history | Opt-in account adapter; not a public trader census |
| Kalshi | Public market data; own member orders/trades/portfolio via signed key | Member-consented account data only; no public trader cohort |
| Gemini Predictions | Public markets/volume; private account positions and order history | Opt-in account adapter; no public trader census |
| Manifold | Public user/bet/portfolio API and research data dumps | M$ is virtual money; separate non-credit research cohort; commercial use requires licensing |
| Prediction.com | Cross-venue event matching and market prices | Discovery only; no wallet-level trader history |
| Bitquery | Decoded Polygon/BNB fills, resolutions and settlement events | Independent chain reconciliation; not another trader cohort |

AQRA must evaluate each venue separately. Normalize realized cash P&L, fees, settlement state, open positions, cash flows and source coverage before comparing features. Do not treat M$, volume, incentives, unsettled marks or balance changes as cash profits. No non-Polymarket data are currently included in AQRA's benchmark. See [Multi-market data study](/research/multi-market-data) for endpoints, access limits and the collection sequence.

**Collection status:** one venue (Polymarket) is currently measured. OPINION and Predict.fun are the next wallet-history sources, but their API credentials are not configured in this checkout. Limitless, Kalshi and Gemini need borrower-authorized account access. No cross-market sample count or performance result is claimed yet.

## Credit-risk model

For loan (i), principal loss after bond recovery is \(\ell_i=\max(0,EAD_i-hB_i)\), where EAD is outstanding principal, B is the posted bond, and h is an explicit assumed recovery fraction. Expected loss is \(EL=\sum_i PD_i\ell_i\). Default probability, correlation, recovery haircut, cost of capital and reserve remain stress assumptions; the trading benchmark does not calibrate them.

For homogeneous loans, correlated defaults are stress-tested with a beta-binomial loss distribution. VaR and Expected Shortfall are calculated from the discrete portfolio-loss distribution. AQRA keeps pooled approval rejected until it has mature repayment labels, a calibrated PD model, out-of-time validation and committed loss capital. A full risk specification is in [Risk engine](/specifications/risk-engine).

## Arkiv integration and current evidence

Arkiv is in the Lend screen. AQRA queries the `aqra.trader.credit.v2` Rabdi schema, then reconciles borrower-owned entities to the EVM lending contract. Arkiv stores expiring discovery pointers. Swarm stores the consented report bytes. Loan principal, bond, repayment and report commitment remain on the lending chain.

On 6 November 2026, the current checkout queried the public Rabdi endpoint and received zero AQRA credit entities. The SDK warned that no access key is configured and public requests are rate-limited. This proves a public read responded; no borrower write receipt or second-wallet read proof is recorded. The workspace also lacks Bee URL and funded Swarm postage, so a new report-backed funded request cannot complete here. Read [Arkiv and Swarm operation](/operations/evidence) and [setup instructions](https://github.com/debojyoti10CC/floorprt/blob/main/arkiv/swarm.md).

## Limitations

- The benchmark is a sampled Polymarket dataset, not a venue-wide census; selection and survivorship bias remain.
- No AQRA loan has matured into a repayment/default validation dataset. P&L is not repayment or collateral.
- A lender can lose principal beyond the bond. The protocol does not custody prediction-market positions.
- The prototype has no independent audit, production pool, or legal clearance.
- CRE workflow source exists, but no successful public CRE/DON execution receipt is recorded.
