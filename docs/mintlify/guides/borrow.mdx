---
title: 'Request a trader-credit loan'
description: 'Review dated wallet evidence, escrow a bond and publish a fixed-term request.'
---

Use the local demo only with loopback Anvil and test USDC. On another configured chain, check the chain and collateral token shown in the wallet before signing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect and load history">
    Open **Borrow**. Connect the borrower wallet, then enter one to four wallet addresses or an ENS name. Select **Check wallet history** to load the as-of Polymarket evidence.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the screen">
    Check the cutoff, linked wallet list, active months and score. Passing the rule allows a request; it does not estimate default probability or guarantee lender funding. ENS discovers addresses but does not prove common ownership.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get linked-wallet consent">
    For every trading wallet controlled by another signer, verify the controller and collect the request-scoped signature. The primary borrower wallet submits the request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose and inspect fixed terms">
    Set principal, bond and term. The prototype caps principal at $100, uses a 2% fixed fee, requires at least a 25% bond and allows 1–30 days. At $100 principal and $25 bond, the due is $102 and up to $75 principal remains short after full bond recovery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve and request">
    Approve the bond amount and sign **Escrow bond and request**. Wait for the confirmed transaction, then review the request in **Your request** or the lender book.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The bond is the only pledged asset. Future trading P&L cannot be collected by the contract. A separate lender must accept the uncovered loss before funding.

## Local contract walkthrough

On local Anvil only, **Run the contract demo** can create a request with a sampled-wallet impersonation, then guide you through the curator report, distinct lender funding and test repayment. This is developer tooling: no production wallet consents and no real-value funds are used. See [Quickstart](/quickstart).
