---
title: 'Review and fund a request'
description: 'Inspect the report and shortfall before advancing principal as a direct lender.'
---

1. Connect a wallet that is different from the borrower.
2. Open **Lend** and refresh the onchain request list, or find a request by ID.
3. Check borrower address, report status, evidence digest, principal, fixed due, bond and deadline.
4. Review **Shortfall after full bond recovery**. At $100 principal and a $25 bond, the lender may lose $75 principal even if the entire bond transfers. Lower recovery can increase the loss.
5. Check the loss-acceptance box, approve the exact principal and choose **Fund**.
6. Wait for the confirmed funding receipt and refresh the request state.

There is no shared lending pool. Each request has one direct lender. The benchmark score is a research screen, not a borrower's PD or guarantee.

If the borrower repays the fixed due by maturity, the bond is returned. If the deadline passes unpaid, any connected wallet can trigger matured-default handling; the contract transfers the bond to the lender and records the remaining principal shortfall.
