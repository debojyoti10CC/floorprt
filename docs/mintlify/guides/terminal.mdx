description: 'The four current product destinations and what each one reads or writes.'
---

| Destination | Purpose | State and actions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Data** | Review the Polymarket sample, forward trader outcomes, dated wallet evidence and benchmark export. | Reads the checked-in replay. It does not estimate repayment probability or lender return. |
| **Risk engine** | Stress explicit default, correlation and bond-recovery assumptions. | Shows model output and keeps pooled credit at `REJECT` until loan labels, validation and committed capital exist. |
| **Borrow** | Resolve a wallet or ENS group, review the as-of evidence, choose fixed terms and request credit. | Linked wallet controllers consent; the borrower wallet approves and escrows the bond. |
| **Lend** | Review an attested request and fund or wait. | A distinct lender acknowledges the uncovered principal shortfall before funding. Repayment and matured default are contract state. |

## Current demo terms

The local Anvil demo uses $100 test-USDC principal, $102 due and a $25 bond. Assuming full bond recovery, the lender still has $75 of principal exposed to loss. If the bond token or contract cannot deliver, the loss can be greater. Trader performance is not collateral.

## Earlier product direction

The original Floor Protocol terminal described markets, an outcome graph, portfolio composer, proof inspector, credit desk, dashboard and settlement explorer. Those seven screens are not the current AQRA application.
