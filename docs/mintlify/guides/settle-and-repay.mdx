## Terminal settlement

1. In Operations, inspect the common observation. Local test resolution requires the configured resolver and timestamp; cancellation is permissionless only at the longstop.
2. On Anvil, **Advance local clock & finalize** explicitly mines a future block. This may mature other observations. A scenario slider in the studio does not write oracle state.
3. Open **Settlement audit** (`/settlements/`), select a funded loan with terminal observation and call **Settle loan** from any connected wallet.
4. Inspect `LoanSettled(redeemed, lenderPaid, borrowerPaid)` and transaction/block/log identity. The residual recipient is the immutable beneficiary, not necessarily the caller or borrower owner.
5. Refresh and reproduce settled state from the chain.

With one-unit canonical collateral, the inside state redeems 2 test USDC and pays 0.78 / 1.22. Outside/upper equality/shared cancellation redeems 1 and pays 0.78 / 0.22.

Shortfall, unresolved observation, already-paid loan or failed transfers revert. Do not retry a pending transaction as a new send before checking its receipt.

## Full fixed-due repayment

Connect the borrower, open **My portfolio** (`/portfolio/`) and select **Repay** on a funded borrower loan. The flow approves the full due if needed, then calls `CreditDesk.repay`.

- All agreed principal and fee remain due; no prorating or early-payment discount.
- Payment goes directly from borrower to lender.
- The exact original claims return to the **position owner**.
- Loan becomes REPAID and position becomes RELEASED.
- Repayment may occur after maturity or oracle finalization while the loan is still FUNDED.
- New-funding pauses do not block repayment.

Settlement and repayment are mutually exclusive: the first successful transaction prevents the other. Failed ERC-20 transfer or receiver callback rolls the entire operation back.

## Unfunded inventory

An OPEN position has no loan to settle or repay. The owner may release it, including after observation maturity, then redeem eligible wallet claims after finalization/cancellation. Whole wallet complete sets can only merge before observation time.
