## Borrower repayment

Before maturity, connect the borrower wallet and choose **Repay** on the funded request. The borrower pays the full fixed due; the escrowed bond returns to the borrower. No installment, discount or partial repayment is supported. Verify the confirmed transaction and `Repaid` state.

## Matured default

After maturity, an unpaid loan becomes eligible for **Settle default** from any connected wallet. The contract transfers the escrowed bond to the lender and records principal loss beyond the bond. This is bond recovery, not liquidation of a prediction-market position. The lender may still lose principal.

## Local demonstration

The local seeded-wallet walkthrough adds a labeled **Repay from local demo borrower** action after the separate lender funds. It impersonates only on loopback Anvil and pays with test USDC. A locally advanced clock is an explicit test operation; it changes state in the local chain and is not part of the production path.
